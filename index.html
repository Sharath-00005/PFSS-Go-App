Employee Phone Installation Guide

Deploy as a Home Screen app with your official crest icon

Step 1 — Host 3 Files Together over HTTPS

Place geoattend.html (renamed to index.html), icon-192.png, and icon-512.png inside one folder, then drag-and-drop that folder onto Netlify Drop (app.netlify.com/drop) or publish via GitHub Pages. HTTPS is required for phone GPS & camera access.

Android Phones (Google Chrome)
  1. Send your HTTPS link to employees on WhatsApp and open in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu (⋮) at the top-right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen → Install.
  4. Your Public Force crest icon appears on their Home Screen and opens full-screen.
iPhone / iPad (Safari)
  1. Open your GeoAttend HTTPS link in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share icon (square with upward arrow) at the bottom.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen → Add.
  4. Staff sign in once with their Employee ID (or Mobile) + 4-digit PIN.
